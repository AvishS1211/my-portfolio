About

Curiosity-driven design,
across every domain

3 years of designing products across gaming, edtech, SaaS, and marketing.

I'm Avish — a product designer who's built for variety. I've worked across gaming, edtech, SaaS, and marketing — and each new domain sharpens how I think about users, business goals, and what good design actually needs to do.

I've partnered with teams at 99games, ForeignAdmits, Big Block Theory, and others to design digital products that people want to use and that move real metrics. Whether it's rethinking an existing experience or building something from zero, I go deep fast.

What I bring: the ability to walk into an unfamiliar problem space, ask the right questions, and ship work that balances user needs with business outcomes. I don't need months of ramp-up — curiosity and craft get me there.

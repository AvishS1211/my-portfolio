About

Curiosity-driven design,
across every domain

3 years of designing products across gaming, edtech, SaaS, and marketing.

I'm Avish — a product designer who thrives on exploring new domains. From gaming studios to edtech platforms, SaaS dashboards to marketing tools, I bring fresh perspective and deep curiosity to every project I take on.

I've had the privilege of collaborating with brands like 99games, ForeignAdmits, Smartcue, and others — helping them shape digital experiences that connect with users and drive real results. Whether it's a full website redesign or building a product from scratch, I'm all in.

I believe great design lives at the intersection of empathy and experimentation. Every new domain is a chance to learn, and every project is a chance to make something people genuinely enjoy using.
